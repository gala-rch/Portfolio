Soul Tamer

A critique of screen addiction, after Francis Picabia's “Animal Tamer” (Dresseur d'animaux).

Format
Photomontage — 600 × 809 px
My role
Creation + staging
Tools
Photoshop
Style
Dada

Result

A man with the chest of an eagle and an owl, surrounded by figures whose faces are erased by a screen, scrolling endlessly.

Reference

Francis Picabia, Dresseur d'animaux, 1937

Exhibition

Shown in my second year in a virtual exhibition room built as a group — the work displayed on a vintage computer, with its label right next to it.

What I did

I created and staged the work. The 3D exhibition room was made by a classmate.
