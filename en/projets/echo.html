ECHO

In a world where an elite sees everything through a chip forced on everyone, Eli seizes that power.

Format
Short film, ~10 min
Team of 5 IMAC students
My role
Assistant director, motion design
Tools
After Effects
Year
2025

The film

CAM 00 · ECHO REC
> See my animations in the film · 3:40

Synopsis

An elite sees everything, knows everything, but feels nothing, thanks to a chip forced on everyone. Through a series of chance events, Eli seizes that power.

Despite the warnings of a mentor, a notorious and slightly crazy drug addict, Eli wants out of the system. And ends up seeing everything, knowing everything.

First fascination, then obsession. What Eli sees and feels leads to paranoia, and to a crime witnessed by the whole elite.

Turning to the mentor for help, Eli discovers that the person who mattered most also has this power. The rest is in the film.

Motion design

Two interface elements designed in After Effects to make the chip visible on screen: the tag that flags monitored behavior, and the scanner that confirms the connection. I had proposed a full version; the director preferred a more minimal one for the film, which I adapted.

What I did

Assistant director: shooting schedule and organizing the shoot.

Motion design: designed the chip's ID tag and connection screen in After Effects.

Short film made by a team of five, as part of the IMAC program.
