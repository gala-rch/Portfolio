Baile Cochino

The last scene of a short story: the party is over, and the morning light reveals what the family was trying to hide.

Format
Scale model — interdisciplinary project
Made with an architect and an audiovisual student
My role
Art direction
Source
Short story “Baile Cochino”

I · Inspiration

Luxurious interiors, and tables left behind after the party.

II · Colors and materials

Pastel tones, gold and wood: a set that imitates wealth.

III · Plans

The room in plan, then the same view dressed with its textures.

IV · Sketches

The scene drawn before building it in volume.

V · Furniture and ornaments

Each object chosen for its period: chandelier, armchairs, tapestries, moldings.

VI · Symbols

Metaphors, personifications, allegories: this work on the text is what set the main objects of the scene.

Everything rests on a contrast: the luxury of the party against the filth of what's left of it. From the start, the family tries to look rich. In this last scene, the mask falls.

Metaphors

The candle just blown outthe death of the illusion.

The melted waxthe family's exhaustion and erosion.

Allegories

Personifications

The making

Laser cutting, wood aged with paint, fabrics glued on like period tapestries, hand-upholstered furniture.

Selected The model was chosen by the teacher to illustrate her upcoming book on art direction.

I chose the scene and handled the art direction. The 3D modeling, 3D printing and laser cutting were done by my architecture classmate.
